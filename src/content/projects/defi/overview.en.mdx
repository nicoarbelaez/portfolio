## The problem

A fitness entrepreneur builds personalized meal plans and training routines for clients, working in Google Sheets. The project automates that work to speed up each plan.

## What I built

I worked on the project from August 2024 to August 2025 and built every piece.

- **Client sheet automation.** A Google Apps Script written in TypeScript that reacts to edits: dependent dropdowns (muscle group, exercise and link), per-sheet and per-month configuration, and progress and error alerts.
- **Database in Apps Script.** A second script that keeps the catalog of exercises and intensification techniques and exposes it as JSON from a web app. The client sheet queries it and writes the result into named ranges.
- **AppSheet app.** A no-code application that calculates food exchanges dynamically.
- **Muscle-map API.** An Express service that receives the muscles worked and returns a colored body image as PNG or JPG.

## Results

- Three pieces delivered in one public repository: the sheet script, the database and the API.
- Version 2.0.0 released in November 2024 and 83 commits through August 2025.
- No usage figures published: the project belongs to a private client and I have no verifiable metrics.
