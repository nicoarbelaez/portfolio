AppSalon is a university project (USC). The rest is technical.

## The problem

A beauty salon needs clients to book without friction, staff to see their schedule, and management to control hours, services and reports in one system.

## What I built

- **Guest booking.** A visitor books an appointment and gets a token to check its status.
- **Three roles**: client, staff and admin, each with an area protected by middleware.
- **Staff schedule** with Excel import and export, with a preview before importing.
- **Admin panel**: a service catalog with full-text search, users and roles, working hours and filterable metrics with charts.
- **PDF reports** generated in the background, with history and download.
- **Account security**: email verification and two-factor authentication with recovery codes.
