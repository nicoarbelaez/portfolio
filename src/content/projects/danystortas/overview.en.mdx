## The problem

Danys Tortas is a bakery with several branches that serves its customers over WhatsApp. When hundreds of customers arrive in a single day, answering late is expensive: an order that should take minutes took hours.

## What I built

A system that covers the whole conversation, from the first question to the order.

- **WhatsApp AI agent.** It receives messages through Kommo CRM, understands text, audio, images and PDFs, searches the catalog, builds the cart, calculates delivery and registers the order. When something is not for an agent (a complaint, a fact it does not have), it hands the conversation to a person with a summary.
- **Web dashboard for the team.** Live conversations, a queue of cases for humans, orders, carts, inventory, deliveries, cash and metrics of the agent itself: cost, attributed revenue and the most expensive conversations.
- **Spend control.** A per-business AI budget, daily and monthly caps, and protection against loops with other bots.

The agent runs on Mecateo's AI agent engine, in production at Danys Tortas.
