Living Sports is a fitness social network for Android. We built it as a team: Nicolás Arbeláez with SebasHDV and brayan200j-collab.

## The problem

People who train usually do it alone, with no way to compare progress. Tracking apps store data but give no reason to come back and no credible way to prove a result.

## What we built

- A **feed** with posts, a media carousel, likes and comments.
- **Communities**: create groups, join them and see their leaderboard.
- **Competitions** per exercise. Each participant uploads a video as evidence, and the system validates the entry and builds a ranking with a podium.
- **Notifications**, profiles, following and personal records.

Competitions are on the `develop` branch. Posts go through an Edge Function that uploads the media to Cloudinary.
