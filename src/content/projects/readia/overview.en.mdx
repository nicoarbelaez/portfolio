ReadIA is a university project (USC). What follows is the technical detail.

## The problem

A small company knows it should adopt AI but not where to start or how ready it is.

## What I built

A web app that collects the company profile, generates a tailored questionnaire with AI, and returns three things:

- A maturity diagnostic with an overall score, a radar by pillar (infrastructure, data, talent and governance), a distribution chart and recommendations.
- A roadmap of phases and tasks, drawn as a graph the user can browse and mark up.
- A PDF report with cover, executive summary, pillars, recommendations, roadmap and conclusions.

One account can manage several businesses. Each diagnostic and roadmap is stored and protected by Row Level Security policies.
